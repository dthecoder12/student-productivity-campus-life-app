# Target User

The primary user is a college student who is juggling classes, homework, exams, part-time work, and campus activities. They want a clear view of what needs to be done each day so they don’t miss deadlines or important events.

Their goals are to stay organized, reduce stress, and feel more in control of their time. They need a simple way to see assignments, schedules, and events in one place. They get frustrated when information is spread across different apps and they have to constantly switch between them to figure out what to do next.
