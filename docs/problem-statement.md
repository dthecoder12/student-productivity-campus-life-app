# Problem Statement

College students often struggle to keep track of assignments, exams, work shifts, and campus events across multiple apps and platforms. Important deadlines are easy to miss when schedules are scattered between learning management systems, calendar apps, email, and social media.

This makes it difficult for students to stay organized, manage their time, and balance academic, work, and personal responsibilities. Students need a single place where they can see everything that matters to their day and week without jumping between tools.
